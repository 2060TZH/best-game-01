VOIDLIGHT is a neon arena wave shooter: one small ship against ten waves of geometric hunters. Move with WASD or the arrow keys, aim with the mouse, fire with a click or Space, and dash through danger with Shift or right-click. Chain kills into a combo multiplier up to x8, grab power-ups (triple shot, shield, overdrive, extra life, void bomb), and mind the spinners that shoot back, the darts that telegraph before they lunge, and the bulwarks that split in two when they die. Clear wave 10 and the void is yours. Everything is drawn on one canvas with synthesised WebAudio sound — no files to download, no network, nothing to build.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 in a desktop browser.
